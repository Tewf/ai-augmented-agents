# 0002: The baseline is the maintainer's arrangement

**Decision:** The review compares everything against B1, the maintainer's arrangement of one coordinating session and six owners, described as practice and cited to nothing private.

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-02. **Shape:** [decision record](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/65e42472e2d4cbfd994c2a86e95a47e5dc7301ca/shapes/decision-record.md).

## Context

- The front page asks that the review end on the baseline, and names the arrangement of 2026-10-29 as the candidate.
- The literature has no standard arrangement for a team of people and models. It has an orchestrator-and-workers practice and one controlled scaling study.
- The arrangement's records are private notes. The public text may describe the practice and its counts, and may not cite the notes.

## Decision

- B1 is the baseline. [baseline.md](../evidence/multi-agent-teams/baseline.md) describes it in the maintainer's own terms and marks its counts as secondary.
- It is compared with a central lead that uses explicit briefs and a central check, the arrangement best supported by the sources found.
- The repository keeps its name. The frame step found that the field says team, and the name stays for continuity with the family.

## Consequences

- A reader can check the comparison against the sources, and can check B1's counts only on the maintainer's word.
- **Bad:** B1 has no control group and no token accounting, so a gain over it would be shown by counts alone.
- A later pass that finds a better public arrangement replaces the comparison, not B1.
