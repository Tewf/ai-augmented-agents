# Position: what is known, what is not, and what this repository adds

Step 4 of the review: the known, the unknown and the addition. Keys are in the [bibliography](../bibliography.md).

**Shape:** [position](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/a7f405f26f14cd188291e2a86b630121c4302a94/shapes/position.md)

## Known

- Agreement under a common prior is a theorem (Aumann 1976; Geanakoplos 1982).
- The jury theorem has versions for correlated votes (Ladha 1992; Berg 1993; Kaniovski 2010).
- Batch search has a regret bound within a constant factor of sequential (Desautels 2014; Kandasamy 2018).
- A central check cut error amplification from 17.2 to 4.4 times (Kim 2025).

## Not known, as far as this pass found

- No work measured rho between model raters of one family or of two.
- No work measured lambda for a lead's audit pass.
- No work gave a rate of error per link from a log of hand-overs.
- These are claims about this pass: one reviewer, 11 sources, no Scholar search, and the string for model judges returned nothing relevant.

## Not done here

- Nothing was run. The five results are stated, and four quantities are named.

## What this repository can add, under three conditions

- A log of items with the verdicts of an exact check, for raters of one family and of two, giving rho and the gain of a second family.
- A log of audit passes with the errors before and after, giving lambda.
- Conditions: the counts come from a program, the number of items is stated, and each result names its limits.
- Grade of this position: supported with caveat, as a design; untested.
