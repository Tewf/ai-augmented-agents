# Position: what is known, what is not, and what this repository adds

Step 4 of the review. It keeps "nobody has done this" apart from "we have not done this here". Keys are in the [bibliography](../bibliography.md).

**Shape:** [position](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/a7f405f26f14cd188291e2a86b630121c4302a94/shapes/position.md)

## Known

- The economics of teams and delegation is old and has models (Marschak 1972; Holmström 1982), for people.
- Systems of language-model agents have surveys and one controlled scaling study (Kim 2025).
- A failure taxonomy of such systems exists (Cemri 2025).
- Routing to cheaper models has measured savings (Chen 2023b; Ong 2024).
- Practice has a convention for the brief (Anthropic 2025).

## Not known, as far as this pass found

- No study varies the fields of a brief and measures the result.
- No study prices briefing and checking against the saving of delegating.
- No source asks what a model agent optimises in the economists' sense, and what follows for the game.
- No source counts decisions taken twice or facts written twice across repositories built by several agents.
- These are claims about this pass: one reviewer for the formal sources, 39 sources, a forward search from three seeds, no Scholar or SSRN search.

## Not done here

- The maintainer's arrangement has no control group and no token accounting.
- No brief was varied, and nothing has been run.

## What this repository can add, under three conditions

- A set of counts from real history: decisions taken twice, facts in two places, questions that reached the person.
- A brief template with the fields of Anthropic 2025, tested by removing one at a time.
- A price for delegating: time to brief plus time to check, against the time saved.
- Conditions: the counts come from a program, the template is tested on a stated number of tasks, and every result names its limits.
- Grade of this position: supported with caveat, as a design; untested.
