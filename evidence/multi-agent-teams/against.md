# Against: evidence that cuts the other way

Evidence against a claim of [review](review.md), kept beside it. Keys are in the [bibliography](../bibliography.md).

**Shape:** [against](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/a7f405f26f14cd188291e2a86b630121c4302a94/shapes/against.md)

## Many agents do not reliably win

- Gains on popular benchmarks are often minimal, which is why Cemri 2025 built its taxonomy (secondary, from its abstract).
- Debate did not reliably beat self-consistency or ensembling, though tuned debate sometimes did (Smit 2023, secondary).
- In Kim 2025, sequential planning lost 70.0% against one agent.
- Where one agent already does well, coordination gives diminishing returns (Kim 2025, abstract).

## A report is not an ablation

- The 90.2% of Anthropic 2025 is on an internal eval of its own system, and its brief fields are not tested one by one.
- Cognition 2025 argues from examples, with no data.
- Kapoor 2024 shows that benchmark accuracy without cost hides complexity.

## The economics may not transfer

- Moral hazard needs an agent who dislikes effort. No source shows a model agent does (Q3 stays open).
- [Team theory](../convergence/review.md) assumes a shared goal. Models in one session share a prompt, not a goal.
- The two classics and the book were not opened; their statements here are the textbook ones.

## People with AI can lose

- The combination did worse than the better of the two in decision tasks (Vaccaro 2024, secondary).
- That covers one person with one system, not a coordinator with six owners.
