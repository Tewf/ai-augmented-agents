# Baseline: the arrangement this repository is compared against

Step 5 of the review. It names the baseline, says what it measured, and compares it with the best arrangement the review found. The facts about the arrangement are the maintainer's practice, written down for this review; the counts come from his notes and are secondary here. Keys are in the [bibliography](../bibliography.md).

## B1, one coordinating session and six owners

**B1** is the maintainer's arrangement of 2026-10-29. Six sessions worked six public repositories at once, and one session answered for the maintainer.

| Part | What B1 does |
|---|---|
| Coordinator | every question, choice and report from the six owners goes to one session |
| What it decides | wording, structure, which shape or rule applies, conflicts between two repositories, local commits |
| What it hands up | anything outward under the maintainer's name, anything destructive, any change of configuration; each with options and a recommendation |
| Owners | one per repository, each with its own context |
| Standing orders | an order the maintainer typed in an owner's window outranks the coordinator's relay |
| Checking | a reviewer per repository read it against one rubric; the coordinator re-checked the sharpest claims before judging |

## Compared with the best arrangement found

The best arrangement the review found is a **central lead with explicit briefs and a central check** (Kim 2025; Anthropic 2025). B1 matches it in two parts and differs in three.

| Part | Best found | B1 | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| One lead | central lead (Kim 2025) | one coordinator | none |
| Central check | errors 4.4 times, not 17.2 (Kim 2025) | coordinator re-checks the sharpest claims | partly: no count of what the check caught |
| Brief fields | objective, format, tools, boundaries (Anthropic 2025) | not recorded as a template | the brief was not tested |
| Shared context | share context, avoid divergent decisions (Cognition 2025) | each owner kept its own context | two stale orders |
| Cost accounting | tokens and time (Anthropic 2025) | none | no price for delegating |

## How the repository would be measured against B1

- Count, from the history, decisions taken twice, facts written twice, and questions that reached the person, for B1 and for a variant.
- A variant fills the gaps one at a time: a brief template, then a shared state file, then a count of the central check's catches.
- The review is finished: B1 is named, and the best arrangement found is the comparison.
